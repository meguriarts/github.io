Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

1. Information Collection

Meguri Arts designs games with user privacy in mind. We do not collect personal data that can identify our players. We only use anonymous data such as device IDs for analytics, bug tracking, and overall app performance improvements.

2. Use of Third-Party Services

To serve ads and analyze app crashes, we use trusted third-party services. These services may collect device-specific information as outlined in their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Unity Ads
  • Meta Audience Network (FAN)
  • AppLovin
  • Firebase Analytics
